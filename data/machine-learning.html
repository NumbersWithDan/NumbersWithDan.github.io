Machine Learning & AI Projects

Predictive models, feature work, and the practical side of trying to make them useful

Supervised Learning

Most of the machine learning work I share here starts with supervised models for regression or classification. The interesting part is rarely just the algorithm. It is deciding what problem is worth predicting in the first place.

Unsupervised Learning

I also use clustering, dimensionality reduction, and other unsupervised methods when the goal is to explore structure before making stronger claims.

Deep Learning & Neural Networks

Deep learning shows up here when the project needs it, but I try not to force it into places where simpler methods would do the job better. The theory-heavy side of that work lives in my academic notes.

Model Validation & Testing

Model evaluation matters at least as much as model selection. I pay a lot of attention to validation, data leakage, overfitting, and whether reported performance actually means anything outside a notebook.

Feature Engineering

A lot of real machine learning work is still feature work. Cleaning, reshaping, and choosing the right inputs often does more for a project than swapping one model for another.

Model Deployment & Production

When a model moves closer to production, the questions shift toward reliability, monitoring, and whether the workflow can survive beyond the first demo. I try to keep that practical perspective in view.

About My Machine Learning Work

I prefer models that are strong enough to be useful and clear enough that I can explain why they behave the way they do.
