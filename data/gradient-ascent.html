Gradient Ascent

A multiplayer climbing game built for an AI agent competition

I built Gradient Ascent for an AI agent competition: a small Foddian-style multiplayer game about climbing higher and trying not to lose the ground you just gained. It brings the tension of a difficult ascent into a shared arena, where a well-timed jump can move you ahead and a bad landing can send you back down.

Play Gradient Ascent

Climb together. Compete for height.

Create a room, share its code, and bring in 2–8 players. Compete for the highest settled altitude after a set number of rounds, or choose a race to the summit.

  • Choose your risk. Higher jump tiers offer more height, but cost compute and demand more precise timing.
  • Time the launch. Lock the angle, then the power. Momentum, bounces, and platform surfaces affect where you land.
  • Use your turn carefully. Jump, place a local trap, or fire a pulse at an opponent. Each action comes at the expense of another.
  • Protect your progress. There are no checkpoints. A fall can erase a good climb.
