My Career Journey

From tutoring to analytics, with a lot of learning along the way

Professional Work

I'm a Data and Analytics Analyst at Fisher Investments in Plano, Texas. I joined in December 2021 as an Associate, then moved into Specialist and Analyst roles.

I use SQL, R, and Python to answer business questions, and build dashboards and reports in Power BI and Excel. I enjoy finding patterns in data and automating tasks that take up too much of people's time.

Before Fisher, I spent about two and a half years at BDO USA in McLean, Virginia, working in Industry Specialty Services. I worked with clients in finance, healthcare, and federal contracting on projects including fraud detection, contract compliance, and dashboards in Power BI and Qlik Sense. That experience taught me a lot about explaining results to people with different backgrounds.

Education

I completed my M.S. in Mathematics with a Data Science concentration at East Texas A&M University in fall 2025. I'm also pursuing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Texas while working full-time.

Before graduate school, I earned my bachelor's degree at Texas A&M University-Texarkana. While I was there, I worked as a math and physics tutor from 2017 to 2019, helping students one-on-one and leading group review sessions. I still enjoy helping people work through technical ideas.

School has given me room to try new things, including health analytics, NBA salary prediction, and log analysis with PySpark. I like being able to connect what I learn in class with the work I do every day.

Tools I Use

Most of my work is in Python, R, SQL, Power BI, and Excel. I've also used Qlik Sense, Spark, Hadoop, AWS, and Jupyter. I pick the tools based on what the project needs and try to keep things straightforward.

What's Next

I want to keep learning and building useful things. I'm always happy to talk about analytics, math, sports projects, or ways to make technical ideas easier to understand.
