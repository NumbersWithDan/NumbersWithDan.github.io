Family & Personal Life

A few things that matter to me away from work, school, and project pages

Personal Life & Family

Most of my public work revolves around analytics and mathematics, but day to day I care just as much about staying connected to the people around me. Time with family and friends is the easiest way for me to reset, laugh a little, and remember that not every problem needs a spreadsheet.

I am lucky to have people in my corner who have always encouraged learning, ambition, and curiosity. That support has mattered through school, work, and the many side projects that somehow keep finding their way onto this site.

Hobbies & Interests

Outside of work, I gravitate toward sports, being outdoors, and anything that gets me away from a screen for a while. I follow basketball closely, still enjoy tennis, and have made peace with the fact that pickleball is fun. Rock climbing remains in the category of "aspirational consistency."

Learning & Growth

Learning is one of the constants in my life. Sometimes that looks like graduate coursework and sometimes it looks like chasing a question just because it seems worth understanding better. The overlap between different interests is usually where I have the most fun.

Community Involvement

Teaching and helping people make sense of difficult material has been part of my life for a while. If someone is trying to figure out math, analytics, or what a career in this space can look like, I'm usually happy to talk.

About This Site

I use this site to share projects, class notes, and side explorations that sit at the intersection of work, school, and curiosity.
