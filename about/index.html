About Me

The background behind the projects, notes, and side interests on this site

Personal Life & Family

Most of this site is about the work, but I did not end up here by accident. I like problems that reward patience, whether that means working through a proof, cleaning up a messy dataset, or trying to understand why a sports story doesn't match the numbers. Outside of work and school, I spend time with family and friends, follow the NBA, play tennis when I can, and admit that pickleball has earned a place in the rotation.

Professional Career

I work in analytics professionally and spend a lot of my time outside of work studying mathematics, statistics, and machine learning. That mix is a big part of how I approach problems: I like the theory, but I also like seeing where it becomes useful in the real world.

Reading List & Learning

Reading has always been one of the easiest ways for me to keep learning. I tend to bounce between math, statistics, analytics, and the occasional book that makes me rethink how people make decisions. Some books are practical, some are technical, and some are there just because they send me down a good trail.

Career History

My career has moved through tutoring, consulting, and analytics roles, but the thread running through all of it has been the same: I enjoy taking something complicated, understanding it well, and explaining it clearly enough that someone else can use it. That's as true for dashboards and reporting as it is for teaching and writing.

Miscellaneous Interests

Beyond data and math, I keep coming back to sports, cities, environmental questions, and the ways people make decisions. Those interests show up all over this site because they are usually where the most interesting projects start.

About This Site

I use this site to share projects, class notes, and side explorations that sit at the intersection of work, school, and curiosity.
