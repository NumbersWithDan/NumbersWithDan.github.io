Interests & Miscellaneous

A few of the subjects and hobbies that tend to turn into projects

Sports & Recreation

Sports are still one of the easiest ways to get me interested in a dataset. I follow the NBA closely, which is part fandom and part an excuse to think about strategy, player development, and decision-making. Away from the numbers, I still like getting out to play tennis and have reluctantly admitted that pickleball is a good time.

Urban Planning & Environmental Science

I am also drawn to questions about cities, heat, infrastructure, and how design choices show up in everyday life. The Dallas heat island project came out of that interest, but more broadly I like problems where data can help explain why one neighborhood feels different from another.

Technology & Innovation

I keep up with machine learning, analytics tools, and the broader technology space, but I care less about chasing every new trend than I do about understanding which tools are actually useful. The interesting part, for me, is seeing when a new method changes how we work and when it is mostly noise.

Community & Learning

I like being around people who enjoy learning in public. Conversations with classmates, coworkers, and other people in analytics tend to sharpen my thinking, especially when we are all trying to make sense of the same problem from different angles.

Connecting Interests

The common thread is that I like subjects where numbers help tell a fuller story instead of replacing it. That could mean basketball, city planning, climate questions, or a project that starts as a class assignment and turns into something much bigger.
