Player Statistics & Performance Metrics

Trying to measure player value without pretending one number can do the whole job

Advanced Player Metrics

Player evaluation gets more interesting the moment you move beyond points per game. I use this section to look at efficiency, role, context, and the tradeoffs involved in boiling a player down to a few useful numbers.

Player Development Analysis

I also like looking at how players change over time: which skills stabilize, which ones grow, and how team context can speed up or stall that development.

Clutch Performance

Clutch performance is one of those topics where the stories get loud quickly. The numbers do not always settle the argument, but they do help narrow down what is real and what is mostly memory.

Injury Impact Analysis

Injuries complicate player evaluation in obvious and not-so-obvious ways. I am interested in how performance changes after time away and what patterns show up in longer recovery arcs.

Positional Analysis

The league keeps stretching the meaning of position, so I like looking at what skills matter most for different roles and how those roles keep shifting.

Player Comparison & Evaluation

Comparing players across teams, roles, and eras is always imperfect, but it is still worth doing carefully. The challenge is finding comparisons that are fair enough to teach you something.

About My Player Analytics

I am less interested in finding a perfect metric than in finding metrics that are useful and honest about what they miss.
