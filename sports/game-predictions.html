NBA Player Salary Predictions

One of my favorite projects in this section uses machine learning to estimate NBA player salaries from performance data. It is part sports project, part modeling exercise, and part attempt to see where basketball value lines up with market value.

Game Outcome Prediction

Predicting game results sounds straightforward until you start stacking up matchup effects, rest, injuries, home court, and all the noise that makes sports fun in the first place.

Player Performance Forecasting

I am also interested in forecasting individual performance, especially when context matters as much as raw talent. Usage, role, matchup, and recent form all change what a "normal" game looks like.

Injury Risk Assessment

Injury-related work is less certain by nature, but it is still worth studying how workload, travel, and playing style interact with availability over time.

Draft Prospect Evaluation

Prospect evaluation is another area where models can help, especially when the goal is to compare players across different systems, roles, and levels of competition.

Trade Value Analysis

Trade value questions are messy in a good way. Performance matters, but so do contract details, age, roster fit, and what a team is actually trying to build.

About My Predictive Analytics

I like predictive work best when the model is useful, the assumptions are visible, and the misses teach you something too.
