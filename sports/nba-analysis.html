NBA Data Analysis

Looking at the game through strategy, team context, and the numbers that actually move the story forward

Team Performance Analysis

I use NBA data analysis to study how teams play, not just what their records say at the end of the season. That includes style of play, efficiency, lineup choices, and the tradeoffs teams make on both ends of the floor.

Shot Selection & Efficiency

Shot profile is one of the easiest ways to see how the league has changed. I like looking at where shots come from, how spacing changes those choices, and what it takes for an efficient offense to hold up over time.

Lineup Analysis

Lineup data gets interesting when it shows which combinations actually work together and which ones only look good on paper. Context matters a lot here, so I try to keep the basketball logic close to the statistical analysis.

Game Flow & Momentum

I am also interested in how games shift over time: scoring runs, matchup changes, and the moments where a game starts feeling different before the final score says so.

Advanced Metrics Development

Some of this work is about testing existing metrics and some of it is about building better ways to summarize performance. The goal is not to replace watching the game, just to give it a sharper set of questions.

About My NBA Analytics

I like the part of basketball analysis where the numbers help explain the game instead of flattening it.
